Through this assignment, I learned Rogers’ five diffusion characteristics. Discussing adoption statistics and supplier success stories helped me realize that neither alone justifies adopting AI. I would now examine how AI fits employees’ workflows and include review and correction time when assessing efficiency. Considering a scenario where replies became faster but errors increased clarified why I would adjust and retest before expanding a pilot. In future evaluations, I will check which organizations and applications the evidence actually represents, then assess its relevance to the specific business rather than assuming that widespread use guarantees local value.
